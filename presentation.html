LAPP Products

Integration & Equipment Application

A comprehensive overview of LAPP solutions used in our machinery.

1. Drag Chains

LAPP SILVYN® CHAIN
LAPP's SILVYN® CHAIN systems are designed for advanced cable protection and management in highly dynamic applications. They ensure smooth movement and longevity of cables in automated equipment.

2. Continuous Movement Cables

LAPP ÖLFLEX® SERVO FD
ÖLFLEX® FD cables are highly flexible cables specifically engineered for continuous movement within drag chains. They are exceptionally durable in constantly moving machinery parts.

3. Cabinet & Cable Glands

LAPP SKINTOP® Cable Gland
SKINTOP® cable glands provide secure, strain-relieved, and often liquid-tight cable entry into control cabinets, safeguarding internal components from environmental factors.

4. Control Cabinet Wiring

LAPP ÖLFLEX® UNIPLUS
ÖLFLEX® UNIPLUS single-core cables are ideal for the internal wiring of devices and control cabinets, providing safe and reliable power distribution.

5. Cable Bundling

LAPP KW Plastic Coil
KW plastic coils are used for easy and quick bundling of cables, providing excellent mechanical protection and clean organization within the equipment setup.

6. Circular Connectors

LAPP EPIC® M12
EPIC® circular connectors, such as M12 connectors, are suitable for robust data, signal, and power connections in industrial applications, offering vibration protection and easy assembly.

7. Data & Network Connectivity

UNITRONIC® & ETHERLINE®
UNITRONIC® (Data & Communication Cables) and ETHERLINE® (Industrial Ethernet Cables) are used for reliable laptop connections, data transmission, and seamless network connectivity.
